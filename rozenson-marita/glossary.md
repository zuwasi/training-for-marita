# Glossary

**Active stroke**: synonym for the effective stroke, the pushing phase of the ciliary beat (ch03).

**Antiplectic**: metachronal wave propagating opposite to the beat direction; the dominant type in mucociliary epithelia, explained energetically by the model (ch01, ch05).

**Bunch (of mucus)**: mucus piled atop the layer by successive cilia beating one after another during their effective strokes (ch05).

**Convected derivative (δ/δt)**: frame-invariant time derivative in Oldroyd's equation, including velocity-gradient stress terms; linearized to the material derivative under the model's smallness assumptions (ch03).

**Effective depth (h̄)**: the height above which Blake's average periciliary shear flow exists, (0.4-0.5)h; almost no flow below it (ch02).

**Effective stroke**: the fast, essentially rigid push of a straightened cilium, about (0.1-0.2)T, planar in the x₂-x₃ plane (ch01, ch03).

**Elastic slab**: the post-beat behavior of the mucus: velocity gradients die out and the layer translates nearly uniformly in depth (ch04b, ch05).

**Envelope model**: prior approach replacing the dense ciliary array by the wavy surface of ciliary tips (ch01).

**False period**: spurious repetition in the summed Fourier series caused by aliasing between M and Λ/l; passed by summing (M·A)/T harmonics (ch04b).

**Fundamental solution**: response of the linear (Newtonian-mucus) problem to a unit δ-force pulse; convolved with the real force profile (ch04b).

**Generalized functions**: distribution theory; forced by the C⁰ smoothness of t₀-averaged fields and the δ-sum force; "the main mathematical tool used in this work" (ch03, ch04a).

**Heaviside window (u)**: step function restricting fields to the mucus layer; its Fourier transform U(y) = (e^{iyh₀} − e^{−iy(H−h₀)})/iy encodes boundary values (ch04a).

**Impulse (force-pulse)**: representation of one cilium's effective stroke as a δ-function in time (and x₂, x₃) at average depth h₀, magnitude F₀ matched to the real energy input (ch03).

**Metachronal wave**: phase-lag wave across the array; Λ = cT; wavelength 20-40 μm in mammalian airways (ch01, ch03).

**Mucus blanket**: the upper viscoelastic layer riding on the ciliary tips (ch01).

**Newtonian viscosity (μ₂)**: limit of the mucus apparent viscosity as shear rate → 0; of minor transport importance given elasticity (ch03, ch05).

**Oldroyd equation**: mucus constitutive law (1 + λ₁δ/δt)τ = 2μ₂(1 + λ₂δ/δt)S with two relaxation times, λ₁ > λ₂; λ₁ = λ₂ is the Newtonian special case (ch02, ch03).

**Penetration**: entry of ciliary tips into the mucus during part of the effective stroke; duration t₀, maximal depth h_p, model-average depth h₀ (ch01, ch03).

**Periciliary layer (serous fluid)**: lower Newtonian layer, viscosity μ₁, depth h, bathing the cilia root to tip (ch01).

**Quasi-steady state**: established periodic regime with constant mean velocity; transients are outside the model (ch03, ch04b).

**Recovery stroke**: the longer return phase, fully submerged in serous fluid (ch01).

**Relaxation times (λ₁, λ₂)**: λ₁ governs stress relaxation, λ₂ the rates of strain; ordering λ₂ ≪ t₀ ≪ λ₁, t₀ ≪ T underpins the whole derivation; λ₁ too large causes mucus rupture (Litt [14]) (ch03).

**Selection rules**: forcing of Fourier modes only at m = kΛ/l (time) and m + s = kΛ/l (space-time), consequences of the metachronal lag (ch04a).

**Sensitivity limitation**: the final equations hide their assumptions; outside the assumed λ-ranges they are invalid, so parameter sensitivity cannot be investigated from the model alone (ch05).

**Serous-mucous structure**: the two-layer medium (Newtonian below, non-linear above) in contact with the beating array (ch01).

**Slender body theory**: low-Reynolds-number superposition of force distributions along cilia (Liron & Meyer [11], Liron & Mochon [12]); fails for non-linear mucus (ch01).

**Symplectic**: metachronal wave propagating in the same direction as the beat (ch01).

**Tangent stress (τ₂₃)**: the only non-zero stress component (with τ₃₂); a running sequence of negative pulses at the beat instants (ch04a, appendices, ch05).

**Zero shear rate viscosity**: see Newtonian viscosity.
